## 1. Business
- **What they are:** Digital ecosystem for vehicle and mobility services in Saudi Arabia. Positioning line: "منظومة متكاملة لخدمات المركبات والتنقل" / "شريكك الموثوق لخدمات المركبات والتنقل".
- **Service map (5 tracks):** 1) شهاب للإصلاح: periodic maintenance, mechanical/electrical faults, body and paint, diagnostics, EV services, 9+ owned centers. 2) اقتناء واستخدام: rental, buy and sell, financing via partners. 3) دعم وعناية: roadside assistance, tires, spare parts, mobile maintenance, car wash, care and protection. 4) بيانات المركبة: inspection and valuation, condition and value reports, telematics (driving behaviour scoring). 5) محفظة شهاب: points and rewards wallet.
- **Stated numbers (from profile, cite as client-provided):** 500K users, 30M rewards distributed, 15+ services, 200K+ vehicles serviced.
- **Vision:** leading ecosystem in the Kingdom. **Mission:** unify technology and data for one integrated experience.
- **Values:** customer experience, digital enablement, service quality, data and innovation, ecosystem growth.
- **Partners (named in profile):** Petromin, Uber, STC Bank, WalaPlus, Tawuniya, Sayara, Al Drees, SASCO, 3M, Tire Ex, Kafarat Plus, Yelo, Lumi, Drive7, Key, OneCard, Noura for Driving, HungerStation, Tree, and others. Mention by name only with written approval.
- **Clients (named in profile):** insurers (SAICO, Salama, Walaa, MedGulf, GIG, Allianz, ACIG), MisMar, GCC Lab, 360 لصيانة السيارات. Same approval rule.
- **Website:** shihab.com.sa
