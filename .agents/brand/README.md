# 3,6T Brand Tokens (from Brand Facelift Guideline v1.0)

Use these for every agency-branded deliverable (proposals, reports, decks, pages). Client work uses the client's identity, not this one.

## Logo
- Wordmark "3,6T" + logotype "تسويق ثلاثمائة وستين درجة / Three Sixty Degree Marketing". Horizontal and vertical lockups.
- Files: `logo-dark.png` (charcoal + turquoise on light), `logo-white.png` (white on turquoise or dark).
- Clear space = 2x the comma in the wordmark. Never tilt, add effects, change proportions, recolor, add gradient, outline, or place on busy backgrounds. White on colored/black backgrounds, black on white is allowed.

## Typography
- Family: **Alexandria** (Arabic and Latin), available on Google Fonts.
- Main headlines 30pt Bold, subheads 20pt SemiBold, body 14pt Regular, captions 12pt SemiBold.

## Pattern
- Grid of slanted parallelograms that thin out into slashes along one axis (fading). Used as a corner or band ornament on stationery, lanyards, and ID cards. Keep it in turquoise on white or white on turquoise, never over text.
