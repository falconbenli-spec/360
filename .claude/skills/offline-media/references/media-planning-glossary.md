# Media Planning Glossary and Formulas

Use these definitions consistently in plans and client reports. Explain them once in the first plan and then use them without re-explaining.

| Term | Definition | Formula or note |
|---|---|---|
| Target audience (TA) | The defined group the plan is measured against | Age, gender, city, language, behavior |
| Universe | Total size of the TA in the measured population | From census or research provider |
| Reach (1+) | Percent of TA exposed at least once during the flight | Net, not gross |
| Effective reach (3+) | Percent of TA exposed at or above the effective frequency | Set the threshold per objective |
| Frequency | Average number of exposures per reached person | GRPs divided by reach |
| GRP | Gross rating points: total exposures as a percent of the TA, double counting repeats | Reach percent times frequency |
| TRP | GRPs measured against the specific TA rather than all adults | Use TRPs for planning when data exists |
| Impressions / contacts | Total exposures in absolute numbers | GRPs times universe divided by 100 |
| CPM | Cost per thousand impressions or contacts | Cost divided by impressions times 1,000 |
| CPP | Cost per rating point | Cost divided by GRPs |
| SOV | Share of voice: the brand's share of category spend or GRPs | Brand divided by category total |
| SOM | Share of market | Compare with SOV for launch planning |
| Flighting | Bursts of activity with gaps | Saves budget, risks forgetting |
| Continuity | Steady presence | Good for always-on categories |
| Pulsing | Base level plus bursts | Common around Saudi seasonal peaks |
| Daypart | Time block for TV and radio | Morning drive, daytime, prime, late night; Ramadan prime shifts later |
| Spot | One airing of a TV or radio ad | 15, 20, 30 seconds standard |
| Sold by GRP vs by spot | Pricing model for TV | GRP deals carry delivery guarantees; spot deals do not |
| Make-good | Compensation for under-delivery or faults | Bonus spots or faces; put it in the contract |
| Proof of posting | Photographic evidence an OOH face is installed | Required for every face |
| As-run log | Station record of actual airings | Audit against the plan |
| Face | One advertising surface on an OOH structure | A unipole has two faces |
| Unipole / megacom | Large roadside OOH structures | Megacom is a very large format common in Saudi |
| Mupi | Street-level backlit panel | Pedestrian areas and malls |
| DOOH | Digital out-of-home screens | Loop length and share of time define exposure |
| Loop | The cycle of ads on a digital screen | A 60-second loop with 6 advertisers gives 10 seconds each |
| Share of time (SOT) | Your share of a DOOH loop | Determines impressions credited |
| Dwell time | Time an audience spends near a placement | Malls and airports high; highways low |
| Visibility adjustment | Factor applied to traffic counts for OOH impressions | Direction, angle, obstruction, speed |
| Geo-lift | Measurement comparing exposed and control geographies | Needs a baseline and comparable markets |
| Post-buy analysis | Comparing delivered GRPs and placements with the plan | Required for every TV and radio buy |

## Quick Formulas

- GRPs = Reach (%) × Frequency
- Impressions = GRPs × Universe ÷ 100
- CPM = (Cost ÷ Impressions) × 1,000
- CPP = Cost ÷ GRPs
- Frequency = GRPs ÷ Reach (%)
- DOOH impressions per day (approximate) = daily audience × share of time × visibility factor

## Planning Heuristics

- Set the effective frequency by objective: new brand launch higher, reminder lower; state the number in the plan and why.
- Plan net reach across media, not the sum of each medium's reach.
- Include production, installation, agency fees, and measurement in the budget before media.
- Build the OOH route from the audience's real journeys (home to work, home to mall, airport road), covering both directions.
- Book Ramadan TV and National Day OOH months ahead; late bookings pay more for less.
- Never quote a rate or audience figure without the current rate card or research deck in hand.
