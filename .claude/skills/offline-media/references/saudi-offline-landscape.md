# Saudi Offline Media Landscape

Orientation for planning. Owners, inventory, and audiences change; verify each item with the current concession holder or sales house before proposing it. Nothing here is a rate or an audience figure.

## Out-of-Home

### Riyadh
- Main arteries: King Fahd Road, Northern Ring Road, Eastern Ring Road, King Abdullah Road, King Abdulaziz Road, Olaya Street, Tahlia (Prince Mohammed bin Abdulaziz), Airport Road, Al Thumamah Road toward the north.
- Districts for premium and B2B: KAFD (King Abdullah Financial District), Olaya, Al Malqa, Hittin, Al Yasmin, Diplomatic Quarter approaches.
- Entertainment and leisure: Boulevard Riyadh City and Riyadh Season zones (seasonal), Diriyah, Riyadh Front, Via Riyadh.
- Malls: Riyadh Park, Kingdom Centre, Granada, Nakheel Mall, Al Hamra, Panorama, Riyadh Gallery, Hayat, U Walk.
- Airport: King Khalid International (KKIA) terminals.
- Transit: Riyadh Metro stations and trains, Riyadh Bus (verify media availability and the concession holder).

### Jeddah and the Western Region
- Main arteries: Tahlia (Prince Mohammed bin Abdulaziz) Street, King Abdulaziz Road, Prince Sultan Road, Al Madinah Road, Corniche, Makkah Road.
- Malls: Red Sea Mall, Mall of Arabia, Al Salaam Mall, Roshan Mall, Jeddah Park.
- Waterfront and Jeddah Season zones (seasonal), Jeddah Yacht Club and Formula 1 area during events.
- Airport: King Abdulaziz International (KAIA) new terminal; Hajj and Umrah traffic seasons.
- Makkah and Madinah: pilgrim audiences, hotel and Haram-adjacent media; content rules are stricter.

### Eastern Province
- Khobar Corniche, King Fahd Road (Khobar), Dhahran-Dammam highway, King Fahd Causeway approaches.
- Malls: Dhahran Mall, Al Rashid Mall, Marina Mall; Aramco-related communities and business audiences.

### Formats
- Unipoles and megacoms (roadside, large, bilingual), digital roadside screens, mupis, bridge banners (verify permitted), mall screens and hanging banners, floor and escalator graphics, lift wraps, airport arrivals and baggage halls, fuel station screens, car and bus wraps.
- Major networks include Al Arabia Outdoor and others holding municipal and mall concessions; confirm the holder per location.

## Television

- MBC Group (MBC1, MBC Drama, MBC Action, MBC2, MBC Iraq not relevant to KSA) with Ramadan series as the premium window; MBC Media Solutions is the group's sales house (verify current structure).
- Rotana channels (Rotana Khalijia for Gulf drama and entertainment).
- SBC and Saudi public channels (Saudi TV, Al Ekhbariya).
- Sports: SSC channels for Saudi Pro League; other rights holders for international sport (verify annually).
- News and business: Al Arabiya, Al Hadath, Asharq News (business audiences, B2B and government relevance).
- Streaming: Shahid (MBC), STC TV, OSN+, YouTube on connected TV; plan with ads but coordinate creative.
- Ramadan: series schedules, sponsorship packages, and product placement sell months ahead; late-night prime shifts to after Taraweeh and toward suhoor.

## Radio

- MBC FM (Saudi-focused, Gulf music and talk), Rotana FM, Alif Alif FM (Riyadh and Jeddah, strong local shows), UFM, Mix FM (English and Arabic pop), Saudi public radio (Riyadh Radio, Jeddah Radio, Quran Radio has no advertising).
- Peak dayparts: morning commute roughly 7 to 10 and evening commute roughly 4 to 8 in Riyadh and Jeddah; Ramadan patterns shift to pre-iftar and late night.
- Formats: spots, presenter live reads, show sponsorships, traffic and weather sponsorships, call-in promotions, on-air competitions (competition permits from the Ministry of Commerce may be required).

## Cinema

- Chains: VOX Cinemas, AMC, Muvi Cinemas, Empire Cinemas, Reel Cinemas (verify presence by city).
- Audience: young adults, families on weekends, premium formats (IMAX, VIP lounges) for affluent segments.
- Sales: by chain or through a cinema advertising sales house; packages by film, by screen, by week; weekend evenings premium.

## Print

- Arabic dailies: Al Riyadh, Okaz, Al Watan, Al Jazirah, Al Madina, Al Eqtisadiah (business), Al Yaum (Eastern Province).
- English dailies: Arab News, Saudi Gazette.
- Sector and business magazines, government publications, in-flight magazines (Saudia's Ahlan Wasahlan, flynas, flyadeal).
- Use: official announcements, tenders, government and B2B credibility, older audiences; verify circulation claims.

## Activations and Venues

- Riyadh Season and Jeddah Season zones (GEA), Boulevard Riyadh City, Diriyah events, AlUla seasons, Red Sea and NEOM event programs.
- Mall activation spaces (book through mall management), exhibition venues (Riyadh Front, RICEC, Jeddah Superdome), sports venues (Kingdom Arena, King Abdullah Sports City).
- Permits: GEA for entertainment events, municipality for public spaces, mall management for in-mall, Ministry of Commerce for promotions and competitions.

## Planning Notes by Objective

| Objective | Priority formats in Saudi |
|---|---|
| National launch | Ramadan or National Day TV plus Riyadh and Jeddah unipole routes, radio frequency, digital retargeting |
| Riyadh retail or F&B opening | Route of boards and DOOH near the location, mall media in the catchment, radio morning drive, Snap Map and geo social |
| Premium or B2B credibility | KAFD and Olaya boards, airport arrivals, business news TV, Al Eqtisadiah and Arab News, LEAP and FII presence |
| Government and semi-government audiences | Print announcements, Al Ekhbariya and Al Arabiya, airport, business districts, official events |
| Youth and entertainment | Boulevard and Riyadh Season screens, cinema, mall DOOH, radio evening, TikTok and Snapchat layer |
| Promotion or sale | Radio burst, DOOH rotation, mall media, WhatsApp and social; permits for discounts |
