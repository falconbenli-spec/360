# Media Owner Brief, Negotiation, and Contract Checklist

## A. The Brief to Media Owners and Agencies

Send one brief to every owner so proposals are comparable.

1. **Advertiser and brand**: who, category, any category exclusivity needed.
2. **Objective**: awareness, launch, promotion, footfall, credibility; the KPI you will measure.
3. **Target audience**: age, gender, cities, districts, language, behaviors (commuters, mall shoppers, business travellers).
4. **Geography**: cities, specific roads, malls, airports, or programs of interest; both directions for roads.
5. **Dates**: flight start and end, phases, must-have dates (Ramadan weeks, National Day, Riyadh Season opening).
6. **Budget**: a range or the deliverables expected (reach, number of faces, GRPs); state whether production is included.
7. **Creative**: formats available (static, digital, bilingual), production lead times, any animation restrictions.
8. **Measurement expectations**: proof of posting, play logs, as-run logs, audience data and its source, post-buy report.
9. **Compliance**: permits needed, content approvals, regulated claims.
10. **Deadline** for proposals and the decision date.

## B. What to Request in the Proposal

- Location list with board IDs, GPS, photos by day and night, traffic direction, nearby obstructions, lighting.
- For DOOH: loop length, number of advertisers, share of time, hours of operation, play log availability.
- For TV: program list, dayparts, spot lengths, guaranteed GRPs or spots, research currency and target definition, make-good terms.
- For radio: station, dayparts, spot positions in break, presenter read availability, show sponsorship details.
- For cinema: chains, cities, films or packages, screens, weeks, admissions data source.
- For print: edition, section, position, circulation claim and its audit source.
- Audience and impression methodology for every number quoted.
- Rate card, proposed net rate, discounts, added value, and production and installation costs itemized.
- Availability calendar and hold policy.
- Cancellation, postponement, and force majeure terms (campaign pauses for national mourning are a real scenario).
- Payment terms and deposit requirements.

## C. Negotiation Levers

| Lever | What to ask for |
|---|---|
| Volume and duration | Package discount for multiple faces, routes, or longer flights |
| Timing | Off-peak discounts; bonus weeks either side of the flight |
| Added value | Extra faces, extra DOOH share of time, bonus spots, social amplification by the owner |
| Production | Printing and installation included, or one free creative change mid-flight |
| Exclusivity | Category exclusivity on a route, program, or mall for the flight |
| Positioning | Specific board IDs, first-in-break positions, program adjacency |
| Guarantees | GRP delivery guarantees with make-goods; downtime credits for DOOH faults |
| Flexibility | Right to shift dates once, pause clause for national events, creative rotation at no cost |
| Payment | Staged payments tied to proof of posting and post-buy report |
| Data | Play logs, as-run logs, and audience reports included in the price |

Negotiate net rates against comparable proposals from at least two owners when the market allows. Record every agreed added-value item in the insertion order; verbal promises do not survive installation week.

## D. Contract and Insertion Order Checklist

- [ ] Exact faces, screens, programs, or positions listed with IDs and dates.
- [ ] Net cost per line, total, VAT shown separately, payment schedule.
- [ ] Production and installation responsibilities and deadlines.
- [ ] Artwork specs, delivery deadlines, and approval process (owner and municipality approvals).
- [ ] Proof of posting within a set number of days of installation; penalty or credit for late posting.
- [ ] Downtime and fault credits for DOOH and backlit boards.
- [ ] GRP guarantees and make-good mechanics for TV; as-run logs for radio and TV.
- [ ] Cancellation and postponement terms, including a pause clause for national mourning or regulatory orders.
- [ ] Category exclusivity terms where agreed.
- [ ] Compliance responsibilities: who obtains which permit; permit numbers to appear in creative where required (real estate, promotions).
- [ ] Reporting: post-buy analysis delivered within an agreed period after the flight.

## E. Post-Buy Audit

1. Compare proof of posting photos against the face list and dates; flag missing or late faces.
2. Reconcile DOOH play logs against the contracted share of time.
3. Reconcile TV and radio as-run logs against the plan; calculate delivered GRPs against guaranteed.
4. Claim make-goods or credits in writing within the contract window.
5. Record the owner's delivery quality in the agency's media notes for the next negotiation.
