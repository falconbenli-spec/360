# Proposal Template (bilingual headings)

Copy this structure. Replace every bracket. Delete sections that do not apply to a small quote, but never delete Situation, Scope, Investment, or Next steps.

---

## Cover

**[Client name]** — [Project or program name]
Proposal prepared by [Agency] · [Date] · Valid until [Date + 30 days]
Prepared for: [Name, title]
Contact: [Account lead name, email, phone]

---

## 1. الملخص التنفيذي · Executive Summary

Three to five sentences. Business goal, the proposed program in one line, the recommended investment tier, the first milestone date.

---

## 2. نظرة على الوضع الحالي · Situation

What we heard in discovery, in the client's words. Cover:
- Where the business is today (revenue trend, channel mix, team)
- What has been tried and what happened
- The specific problem this engagement solves
- Constraints (budget, approvals, season, regulation)

Quote the client where you can: "You told us that ... ."

## 4. المنهجية · Approach
