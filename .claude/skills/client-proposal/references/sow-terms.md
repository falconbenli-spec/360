# Scope of Work and Terms Clauses

Plain-language clauses to adapt. These are drafting aids, not legal advice; the agency's legal counsel reviews the master agreement.

## Scope and deliverables

**Deliverables.** The Agency will provide the deliverables listed in Schedule A in the quantities and periods stated. Deliverables not listed are out of scope.

**Revisions.** Each deliverable includes [two] rounds of revisions. A round is one consolidated set of client comments. Additional rounds are billed at [SAR X per hour / per asset].

**Client dependencies.** The Client will provide approvals, brand assets, product information, and account access within [3] business days of request. Delays extend the Agency's delivery dates by the same number of days.

**Media spend.** Advertising spend is paid by the Client directly to the platforms [or: is billed by the Agency at cost with the agreed management fee]. Media spend is not part of the service fee.

**Third-party costs.** Production, talent, influencer, printing, venue, stock, and tool costs are estimated in writing and incurred only after written Client approval. Billed at cost [plus X% handling].

## Change orders

**Change order.** Either party may request a change to scope, timeline, or deliverables in writing. The Agency will respond within [3] business days with the impact on fees and dates. Work on the change begins after written approval. Requests made verbally or in meetings are confirmed in writing before work starts.

## Fees and payment

**Fees.** Monthly fees are invoiced in advance on the first business day of each month and are due within [15] days. Setup fees are invoiced on signature. Project fees are invoiced [50% on signature, 50% on delivery / 40-40-20 by milestone].

**VAT.** All fees are exclusive of value added tax, applied at the prevailing rate.

**Late payment.** Work may be paused on accounts more than [30] days overdue after written notice. Paused media campaigns resume when payment is received; the Agency is not responsible for performance loss during a pause.

## Term and termination

**Term.** The initial term is [6/12] months from the start date, continuing month to month afterward.

**Notice.** Either party may end the agreement after the initial term with [30/60] days written notice. Fees for the notice period are payable.

**Early termination.** Termination within the initial term requires payment of [the remaining term / a fixed termination fee], unless the Agency is in material breach and fails to cure within 15 days of written notice.

## Intellectual property

**Deliverables.** On full payment, the Client owns the final approved deliverables.

**Agency materials.** Methods, templates, frameworks, tools, and unused concepts remain the Agency's property. Drafts and rejected concepts are not transferred.

**Third-party assets.** Stock, fonts, music, and licensed material are subject to their own licenses, which the Agency will disclose.

**Portfolio.** The Agency may show the work in its portfolio and case studies [after public release / with Client approval], without disclosing confidential metrics.

## Accounts and data

**Account ownership.** Ad accounts, analytics properties, social profiles, and domains are created in the Client's name and remain the Client's. The Agency is granted access, not ownership.

**Data.** Customer data is processed only to deliver the services, in line with the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law. The Agency will not sell, share, or use Client customer data for other clients.

**Handover.** On termination the Agency transfers access, provides exports of campaigns and creative, and documents the account structure within [15] business days.

## Confidentiality

Mutual. Covers business information, data, strategies, and pricing. Survives termination for [2] years.

## Performance

**No guarantee.** The Agency commits to the process, effort, and transparency described in the proposal. Results depend on factors outside the Agency's control, including market conditions, platform changes, product, pricing, and Client approvals. The Agency does not guarantee rankings, sales, followers, or specific results.

**Performance bonus (if used).** A bonus of [amount or %] is payable when [metric] exceeds [target] over [quarter], measured by [tool], with attribution rules agreed in writing before the period begins.
