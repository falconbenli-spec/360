# Discovery Question Bank

Pick from each group. A discovery call runs 45 to 60 minutes; do not ask everything. Record answers in `.agents/clients/<client>.md`.

## Business and goals

- What does the business look like in 12 months if this works? In numbers.
- What is the one metric the owner or board looks at?
- What would make this engagement a failure in your eyes?
- Why now? What changed?
- Where does revenue come from today, by channel, roughly?
- What is the average order value or deal size, and the margin?
- What is the sales cycle, and who is involved in the purchase?

## Audience

- Describe your best customer. Where did they come from?
- Who do you wish bought from you but doesn't?
- What do customers say when they explain why they chose you?
- What objections does sales hear most?
- Which cities or regions matter most? Arabic or English first?

## Competition

- Which three competitors do you lose to, and why?
- Who do you admire in your category, even outside Saudi?
- What do you do that no competitor can say?

## Marketing history

- What have you tried? What worked, what did not, and why do you think so?
- Who did the work: in-house, freelancers, agencies? Why did it end?
- What content, creative, and brand assets exist today?
- What do you spend on media today, by platform?
- What is the best campaign you have ever run?

## Tracking and data

- What analytics are installed? Who has access?
- Are pixels and conversion events set up? Server-side?
- Which CRM? Does marketing data reach it?
- Can you tell today which channel produced a given sale or lead?
- Do you have call tracking, chat, or WhatsApp lead capture?

## Operations and approvals

- Who approves strategy, creative, and spend? How long does each take?
- Are there brand, legal, or regulatory review steps?
- Any sector rules (health, finance, education, food) that constrain claims or creative?
- What is your availability for weekly calls?
- Who on your team will be our day-to-day contact?

## Budget and timing

- What budget range do you have for services, and separately for media?
- Is the budget approved, or does this proposal help get it approved?
- When do you need to see first results? Is there a season or launch driving that?
- Are you talking to other agencies? What will decide it?

## Closing the call

- What did we not ask that we should have?
- If we propose something different from what you asked for, are you open to that?
- When would you like to see the proposal, and in which language?
