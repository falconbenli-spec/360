# Client Health Score Rubric

Score each dimension monthly: Green (3), Amber (2), Red (1). Record in the client file. Overall health is the lowest two dimensions, not the average, because one red dimension can end an account.

| Dimension | Green | Amber | Red |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Results** | KPIs at or above target for 2 of the last 3 months | Below target but trend improving, or one bad month with a known cause | Below target 2 of 3 months with no clear recovery |
| **Engagement** | Attends meetings, approves within the agreed window, replies same day | Occasional delays, delegates meetings | Cancels meetings, approvals stall, no replies |
| **Sentiment** | Compliments, referrals, shares internal wins with us | Neutral, transactional | Complaints, escalations, "disappointed" language |
| **Commercial** | Pays on time, scope stable or growing, budget steady or up | Late payment once, small budget cut with reasons | Repeated late payment, budget cut without discussion, asks about contract terms |
| **Relationship breadth** | Three or more active contacts including the decision maker | One strong champion, limited access above | Champion gone or reorganized, single contact only |
| **Strategic fit** | Client growing, marketing a priority, our services match their next stage | Flat, marketing one of many priorities | Shrinking, marketing deprioritized, needs we do not offer |

## Actions by overall score

- **All green / one amber**: maintain; look for the upsell moment at the QBR.
- **Two ambers**: account lead addresses the specific issues this month; note in the monthly internal review.
- **One red**: save plan within two weeks, agency lead informed.
- **Two or more reds**: agency lead calls the client's decision maker this week; re-audit and re-plan; consider a team change.

## Monthly internal account review (15 minutes per client)

1. Health score and change since last month
2. Open promises and their dates
3. Scope creep log
4. Upsell opportunities with evidence
5. Risks and the owner of each save action
