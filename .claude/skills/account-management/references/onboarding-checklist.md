# Client Onboarding Checklist

Assign an owner and a date to every line at kickoff. Chase daily until complete. Target: all critical items within 10 business days.

## Commercial and legal

- [ ] Signed agreement and schedule A (scope) filed
- [ ] NDA signed
- [ ] Data processing terms (PDPL) agreed if customer data will be handled
- [ ] Invoicing details: legal name, VAT number, address, PO requirements, invoice contact
- [ ] Setup invoice issued
- [ ] Media spend arrangement confirmed (client pays platform directly / agency bills at cost)
- [ ] Third-party cost approval process confirmed

## People and process

- [ ] Stakeholder map completed (decision maker, champion, day-to-day, approvers, finance)
- [ ] Approval workflow agreed: tool, window in business days, silent rule, backup approver
- [ ] Communication channels created (WhatsApp/Slack group, shared folder)
- [ ] Cadence agreed and calendar invites sent (weekly, monthly, QBR)
- [ ] Client working hours, holidays, and blackout dates noted
- [ ] Escalation path agreed on both sides
- [ ] Client context file created at `.agents/clients/<client>.md`

## Access (partner or user roles, never shared passwords)

### Advertising
- [ ] Meta Business Manager: partner access to ad account, page, pixel, catalog, Instagram
- [ ] Google Ads: linked to agency manager account; billing confirmed
- [ ] Snapchat Ads Manager: member role on the ad account
- [ ] TikTok Business Center: partner access to ad account and pixel
- [ ] X Ads, LinkedIn Campaign Manager as applicable
- [ ] Google Merchant Center / product feeds as applicable
- [ ] App campaigns: MMP (AppsFlyer/Adjust) partner access

### Measurement
- [ ] GA4 property: editor access; data retention and filters reviewed
- [ ] Google Tag Manager: publish rights
- [ ] Google Search Console: full user
- [ ] Meta Pixel and Conversions API status confirmed
- [ ] Platform pixels (Snap, TikTok) status confirmed
- [ ] Call tracking or WhatsApp lead capture in place or planned
- [ ] CRM user created; lead source fields exist
- [ ] Rank tracking and SEO tools configured for the domain

### Web, store, app
- [ ] CMS or store admin (Salla, Zid, Shopify, WordPress, custom): appropriate role
- [ ] Developer or hosting contact for tag and code changes
- [ ] DNS contact for domain verifications
- [ ] App Store Connect and Play Console roles if app marketing is in scope

### Social and content
- [ ] Instagram, Snapchat, TikTok, X, LinkedIn, YouTube: admin or partner roles through the business tools
- [ ] Content calendar tool access
- [ ] Previous content and campaign archives shared

## Brand and inputs

- [ ] Brand guidelines, logos (vector), fonts, color codes
- [ ] Photography, product shots, video archive
- [ ] Previous campaigns and their results
- [ ] Product and pricing information, promotions calendar
- [ ] Customer research, personas, surveys, reviews
- [ ] Sector compliance rules and forbidden claims documented
- [ ] Preferred Arabic register and glossary of brand terms
- [ ] Competitor list from the client

## Foundations (weeks 2–4)

- [ ] Marketing audit delivered (see marketing-audit)
- [ ] Tracking plan agreed and implemented (see analytics, conversion-tracking)
- [ ] Baselines recorded in the client file
- [ ] Strategy presented and signed off
- [ ] Early win delivered and reported
- [ ] First weekly update sent (week 2)
- [ ] Day-30 check-in call held; friction fixed

## Internal

- [ ] Team assigned and briefed; roles clear
- [ ] Time tracking category created
- [ ] Reporting template prepared for the client
- [ ] Renewal date and 90-day review in the calendar
