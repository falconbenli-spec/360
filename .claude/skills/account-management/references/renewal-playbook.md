# Renewal Playbook

Renewals are won in the year, not in the last two weeks. The playbook starts 90 days before the term ends.

## Day −90: Prepare

- Health score review: anything red gets fixed now.
- Build the year-in-review: objectives, results, learnings, assets created, problems solved.
- Identify what the client's business needs next year and which services match.
- Decide the renewal shape: same scope, expanded, adjusted, or re-priced. Any price increase needs a reason the client will accept (expanded scope, inflation in costs, proven results).

## Day −60: QBR with the decision maker

Use the QBR outline in client-reporting. Add:
- "The year in numbers" slide: results vs the original objectives.
- "What we built together": tracking, creative library, audiences, learnings. This is the switching cost, stated as value.
- Next year's plan with targets.
- The renewal proposal, verbally first.

Script for opening the renewal conversation:
> "The current term ends on [date]. Before we get there we wanted to review the year properly and propose how we think next year should look. We'd like to keep working with you; here is what we recommend."

Arabic:
> "ينتهي العقد الحالي في [التاريخ]. قبل ذلك أردنا مراجعة السنة معكم واقتراح شكل العمل للسنة القادمة. نرغب في الاستمرار معكم، وهذا ما نقترحه."

## Day −45: Send the renewal document

Short version of the proposal structure:
1. Year in review (results, learnings)
2. Next year's objectives and plan
3. Scope (same / changes)
4. Investment and terms
5. Next steps and dates

## Day −30: Negotiate

- Listen for the real concern: price, results, relationship, internal politics.
- Trades for discounts: longer term, advance payment, case study rights, scope adjustments.
- If budget shrank: propose a smaller scope rather than a discount on the same scope.
- If they want to "test the market": offer a reference call with another client and ask what would make them stay.

## Day −14: Close

- Agency lead to client lead if unsigned.
- Confirm the start date, invoice, and kickoff for the new term.

## Day 0

- Renewed: thank-you note, updated client file (new term, targets, scope), plan kickoff.
- Not renewed: offboarding checklist (see SKILL.md), exit interview, testimonial request if appropriate, 6-month check-in in the calendar.

## Common renewal objections

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "We want to bring it in-house" | Offer a transition plan and a lighter advisory retainer; stay the strategic partner. |
| "Results plateaued" | Agree if true; bring the next-stage plan (new channels, CRO, CRM) with evidence of headroom. |
| "Budget is cut" | Re-scope to the budget; protect the highest-return activities. |
| "New leadership wants their own agency" | Ask for a meeting with the new leader; present the year's results and the switching cost factually; offer a 90-day trial under new terms. |
| "Price increase is too much" | Explain the reason; offer to hold price for a longer term or reduce scope. |
