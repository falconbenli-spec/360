# Quarterly Business Review Outline

A QBR is a strategy conversation with numbers, not a long monthly report. 45 to 60 minutes. Invite the client's decision maker, not only the day-to-day contact.

## Before the meeting

- Pull the quarter's data against the annual objectives.
- Re-read the proposal's objectives and the client file's learnings log.
- Prepare one recommendation that changes something: budget, channel mix, scope, or targets.
- Send the deck 24 hours ahead with a two-line summary.

## Deck (12 to 18 slides)

1. **Agenda and objectives of the session**
2. **The year so far**: annual objectives, quarter results, cumulative progress. One slide, one table.
3. **Quarter scorecard**: KPIs by month, targets, variance.
4. **What we learned** (three to five slides): the insights that change how we work. Each slide: the insight, the evidence, the implication.
   - About the audience (who converts, who does not)
   - About channels (what is scaling, what is saturating)
   - About creative and messaging
   - About the funnel and the product or offer
5. **What did not work and why**: candid. One slide.
6. **Market and competitors**: changes observed in the quarter.
7. **Strategy check**: is the plan from the proposal still right? What changes?
8. **Next quarter plan**: initiatives, expected impact, timeline.
9. **Budget recommendation**: media and services, with the reasoning. If recommending an increase, show the evidence of headroom (marginal returns, untested channels, capacity).
10. **Scope changes** (if any): what to add, remove, or shift. Priced.
11. **Targets for next quarter**: proposed numbers per KPI.
12. **Requests and decisions needed**: a list with dates.
13. **Appendix**: detail tables.

## Arabic framing for the opening slide

> **مراجعة الربع [الأول] – [العميل]**
> الهدف السنوي: [ ]. تحقق حتى الآن: [ ] ([ ]%).
> أهم ثلاث نتائج هذا الربع، وأهم ثلاثة قرارات نحتاجها منكم اليوم.

## Running the meeting

- Spend a third on results, two thirds on learning and the plan.
- Ask the client what changed in their business that you do not know about.
- Get decisions in the room: targets, budget, scope. Record them.
- Close with next steps, owners, dates.

## After the meeting

- Send minutes within 24 hours: decisions, targets, actions.
- Update `.agents/clients/<client>.md`: objectives, targets, learnings, stakeholder notes.
- If scope or budget changed, issue a change order or an updated proposal (see client-proposal).
