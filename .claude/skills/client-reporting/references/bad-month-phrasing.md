# Explaining a Bad Month: Phrasing

Honest, specific, forward-looking. No hedging, no blame-shifting, no burying.

## Verdict lines

English:
- "Leads came in at 62% of target. The cause was creative fatigue on Snapchat in the first half; we corrected it on the 14th and the second half ran 28% below the first half on CPL."
- "Revenue was below target by 18%. Two of the three causes were on our side and are fixed; the third is a stock-out on the top product, which we need to plan around."

Arabic:
- "جاءت النتائج أقل من المستهدف بنسبة 38%. السبب الرئيسي إرهاق الإعلانات على سناب شات في النصف الأول من الشهر، وقمنا بتصحيحه في 14 الشهر، فانخفضت تكلفة العميل المحتمل في النصف الثاني بنسبة 28% مقارنة بالنصف الأول."
- "لم نحقق المستهدف هذا الشهر. سببان من ثلاثة كانا من جانبنا وتمت معالجتهما، والسبب الثالث نفاد مخزون المنتج الأكثر طلبًا، ونحتاج التخطيط له معكم."

## The three buckets

State the bucket for each cause. Example table:

| Cause | Bucket | Evidence | Fix | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Creative fatigue on two ad sets | Agency | Frequency rose past our threshold by day 10; CTR fell 40% | New creative round launched day 14 | Done |
| Approvals averaged 9 days vs 3 agreed | Client | Approval log | Propose a backup approver | Needs decision |
| Snapchat CPM rose in the last ten days of Ramadan | External | Platform CPM trend | Shifted budget to search for the period | Done |

## Taking responsibility

- "We should have caught the fatigue by day 7; our monitoring threshold was too loose. We tightened it to a weekly creative check."
- "The tracking break on the 3rd was ours. Conversions from the 3rd to the 6th are under-reported; we have estimated the gap in the appendix and marked it clearly."

Arabic:
- "كان يجب أن نلاحظ الإرهاق الإعلاني مبكرًا. عدّلنا آلية المتابعة لتكون فحصًا أسبوعيًا للمحتوى الإعلاني."

## Raising a client-side cause without blame

- "Approvals took longer than the three days we agreed, which delayed the mid-month launch by a week. Could we agree a backup approver for next month so launches hold their dates?"
- "The pricing change on the 12th coincided with a drop in conversion rate. It may be worth testing the previous price on a segment to confirm."

Arabic:
- "استغرقت الموافقات أكثر من المدة المتفق عليها مما أخّر إطلاق الحملة أسبوعًا. نقترح تحديد شخص بديل للموافقة الشهر القادم للحفاظ على مواعيد الإطلاق."

## Committing to recovery

- "Next month's realistic target is 85% of the original, rising to 100% in the month after once the new creative has a full month and approvals are back within the agreed window."
- Avoid: "We are confident next month will be much better." Replace with a number and a condition.

## Phrases to avoid

- "Market conditions" without a specific condition named
- "The algorithm changed" without evidence
- "Unfortunately" more than once per report
- Any benchmark without a source
- "Slight" or "minor" for a miss larger than 10%
