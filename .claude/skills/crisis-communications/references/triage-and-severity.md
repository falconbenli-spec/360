## Severity Level Table

| Level | Name | Triggers (any one) | Response window | Who is activated | Statement |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Local | A few complaints; no reposts by accounts larger than the client; no media; no harm beyond inconvenience | Same business day | Community manager | Direct reply; private resolution |
| 2 | Spreading | Reposts by accounts with reach larger than the client's; a thread or clip gaining replies hour over hour; sentiment turning; a creator with a loyal audience involved | 1 hour to holding statement | Agency lead, client decision-maker, spokesperson on standby | Holding statement pinned on channel of record |
| 3 | Public | Any media coverage; trending hashtag; a regulator, ministry, or official account engaged; financial loss to customers; data exposure; physical injury | 30 to 60 minutes to statement; updates at stated intervals | Full crisis team, legal, client leadership | Formal statement, Q&A for staff, media line open |
| 4 | Existential | Licence or regulatory action threatened; criminal dimension; death or serious injury; national or cultural sensitivity; sustained coverage over 48 hours | Immediate; leadership visible within hours | All of Level 3 plus CEO, board, government relations | Leadership statement on camera or in person, written statement, ongoing updates |

A level can rise fast. Re-classify every two hours in Level 2 and every hour in Level 3.

## Velocity and Reach Signals

Measure, do not guess. Record these at triage and at each review:

- **Volume:** number of posts mentioning the incident in the last hour, versus the hour before.
- **Velocity:** up, flat, or down over the last three readings.
- **Amplifiers:** the five largest accounts that have posted or reposted, with follower counts.
- **Crossover:** has it moved platform (TikTok to X, X to news site, X to WhatsApp forwards reported by staff)?
- **Media:** any outlet, including small news accounts and aggregator accounts on X, which often precede mainstream pickup.
- **Official engagement:** any ministry, authority, or verified official account replying or being tagged repeatedly.
- **Inbound:** customer-service volume versus a normal day, and the top three themes.

A rough rule for Saudi consumer brands: if the incident thread has more engagement than the client's best post of the month, it is at least Level 2.

## Category-Specific Triggers

| Client category | Watch for | Likely regulator or body to name (verify current rules) |
|---|---|---|
| Food and beverage | Foreign object, illness claims, hygiene videos, expired products | Saudi Food and Drug Authority; municipality inspectors; Ministry of Commerce |
| E-commerce and retail | Non-delivery at scale, counterfeit claims, refund refusals, price-labelling complaints | Ministry of Commerce; e-commerce rules under the Ministry of Commerce |
| Fintech and payments | Failed transactions, unauthorized charges, outages | Saudi Central Bank (SAMA) |
| Telecom and apps | Outages, data exposure, privacy claims | Communications, Space and Technology Commission; Saudi Data & AI Authority for personal data |
| Healthcare | Patient harm, misdiagnosis claims, privacy | Ministry of Health; Saudi Food and Drug Authority for products |
| Real estate | Delayed handover, contract disputes, misleading ads | Real Estate General Authority |
| Education | Safety of students, fee disputes | Ministry of Education or relevant authority |
| Any | Employee conduct videos, discrimination claims, cultural or religious offence, political comments by staff | Ministry of Human Resources for labour matters; otherwise reputational |

Tell the client to confirm notification obligations and timelines with counsel. Never state a statutory deadline from memory.

## War Room Setup

One group chat (WhatsApp or Teams) named for the incident, and one shared document with these sections:

1. Facts: confirmed / being verified / false, each with a timestamp and source.
2. Decisions log: who decided what, when.
3. Approved statements: current version only; older versions moved to an archive section.
4. Monitoring readings: every review, in a table.
5. Media inquiries: outlet, journalist, question, deadline, who answered, what was said.
6. Actions and owners with due times.

Roles, each a named person with a backup:

- **Decision-maker** (client): approves statements and actions.
- **Agency lead**: runs the room, keeps the clock, enforces the process.
- **Comms writer**: drafts and revises every statement.
- **Spokesperson**: the only external voice.
- **Legal**: reviews, strikes, flags obligations.
- **Monitoring owner**: readings and alerts.
- **Customer-service lead**: scripts, inbound themes, direct resolutions.
