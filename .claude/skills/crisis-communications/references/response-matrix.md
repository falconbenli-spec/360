## Decision Table

| Situation | Reach | Legitimacy | Action | Where | By when |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Troll or bait | Any | None | Ignore and log | None | n/a |
| Clearly false claim, no traction | Low | None | Ignore and log; prepare a correction in case it grows | None | Prepare within 2 hours |
| Clearly false claim, growing | Medium to high | None | Correct once with evidence | Channel of record, then reply on the original thread with a link | 1 hour |
| Real complaint, one person | Low | Real | Resolve privately, acknowledge publicly in one line | DM plus a short reply on the thread | Same day |
| Real complaint, pattern (several people, same issue) | Medium | Real | Public acknowledgment plus a fix timeline | Channel of record; reply on the biggest threads | 2 hours |
| Real complaint, viral | High | Real | Holding statement, then a resolution statement | Channel of record; pinned | 1 hour |
| Creator or influencer complaint | Depends on their audience, not their follower count | Real | Direct contact with the creator first, then public | DM or call, then their thread | 1 hour |
| Employee conduct video | Medium to high | Real | Acknowledge, state the investigation, do not pre-judge the employee publicly | Channel of record | 2 hours |
| Safety, data, financial harm | Any | Real or alleged | Escalate to Level 3 | Channel of record, media line | 30 to 60 minutes |
| Journalist inquiry | n/a | n/a | Escalate to public-relations; written answers; respect the deadline | Email or call | Before their deadline |
| Competitor smear | Any | Fabricated | Do not name the competitor. Correct the fact if it spreads; otherwise ignore | Channel of record if needed | 2 hours if responding |
| Regulator or official account engages | Any | n/a | Level 3; respond formally and privately first; acknowledge publicly | Formal channel, then public | 1 hour |

## Reply Patterns

### Acknowledging a single legitimate complaint (public, on the thread)

Arabic:
```
أهلاً [الاسم]، نعتذر عما حصل. أرسلنا لك رسالة خاصة لنحل الموضوع اليوم.
```
English:
```
Hi [name], we're sorry about this. We've sent you a direct message so we can resolve it today.
```

Do not ask them to "DM us" and leave it there. Send the DM first, then say you did.

### Acknowledging a pattern

Arabic:
```
وصلتنا عدة بلاغات عن [المشكلة]. نعمل على حلها الآن، ونتوقع الانتهاء خلال [المدة].
من تأثر: تواصلوا عبر [القناة] وسنعوضكم مباشرة.
```
English:
```
We've received several reports about [issue]. We're fixing it now and expect to be done within [timeframe].
If you were affected, contact us at [channel] and we'll make it right directly.
```

### Correcting misinformation (once)

Arabic:
```
توضيحاً لما يتم تداوله: [الادعاء] غير صحيح. [الحقيقة بجملة واحدة، مع الدليل إن وجد].
نرحب بأي استفسار عبر [القناة].
```
English:
```
To clarify what is being shared: [claim] is not accurate. [The fact in one sentence, with evidence where available].
We welcome any questions at [channel].
```

Post it once. Do not reply to every quote-tweet with the same text; it reads as a bot and extends the thread's life.

### Replying to a creator

Call or DM, within the hour, from a named senior person at the client (not the agency, not a generic brand account):

```
أهلاً [الاسم]، أنا [الاسم والمنصب] في [العلامة]. شفت اللي صار وأعتذر لك شخصياً.
أبي أفهم التفاصيل منك مباشرة وأحلها اليوم. ممكن أتصل بك؟
```

No offers of gifts or money in exchange for deleting content. It leaks and it becomes the second crisis.

### Employee conduct

Arabic:
```
اطلعنا على المقطع المتداول ونأخذ ما ورد فيه بجدية.
فتحنا تحقيقاً داخلياً وسنتخذ الإجراء المناسب وفق نتائجه.
سلوك كهذا لا يمثل قيمنا، ونعتذر لمن تأثر به.
```
Do not announce the employee's dismissal in the first statement. Investigate, then act, then say what was done.

## Platform-Specific Handling

**X**
- Reply on the original post, not only on the client's own timeline.
- Pin the statement on the profile for the duration.
- Turn off scheduled posts. Pause promoted posts and ads targeting Saudi.
- Do not restrict replies on the statement; it reads as hiding.
- Do not block complainants during the incident unless they threaten or abuse.

**Snapchat**
- Monitor through creator relationships and tagged mentions; there is no hashtag feed to watch.
- Respond to creators privately. Do not post a story rebuttal; it reaches your audience, not theirs, and signals a fight.
- If the client has a public profile, a calm story pointing to the statement is enough.

**TikTok**
- Reply once in the comments of the viral clip with a short, calm acknowledgement and a pointer to the statement. Do not stitch or duet the complaint.
- Expect the clip to be reposted on X within hours; have the X response ready.

**Instagram**
- Comments on the latest posts will fill with the incident. Reply to the top ones with the acknowledgement; do not hide or delete comments except abuse or personal data.
- Pause scheduled Reels and promotional stories.

**Google Business Profile and app stores**
- Reply to incident-related reviews after the statement is out, with the same wording, in Arabic.

**WhatsApp**
- Cannot be monitored. Equip customer service and front-line staff with the correct statement to forward back, and ask store or branch managers to report what customers are showing them.

## What Never To Do

- Delete the original complaint or earlier replies.
- Respond with the brand's playful voice, emojis, or humour.
- Say "no comment" to a journalist.
- Blame the customer, a supplier, or a "technical glitch" without evidence.
- Promise a refund or compensation timeline before operations confirms it.
- Let the founder or employees post personal takes on the incident.
- Launch a promotion or discount "to make up for it" during the incident; it reads as buying silence.
- Reply to every single post with the same text.
- Mention a competitor by name, ever.
- Post a long legalistic statement with no concrete action and no contact point.
