# [Client] — [Campaign name] · بريف الحملة
**Date:** [ ] · **Account lead:** [ ] · **Creative lead:** [ ] · **Media lead:** [ ] · **Version:** [ ]

## الخلفية · Background
Three sentences: where the business is, what triggered this campaign, what has been tried before and what happened (from the client file learnings log).

## الهدف · Objective
One objective. KPI, target number, date.
Example: 4,000 online orders between 1 and 30 Ramadan, measured in the store's completed orders, up from 2,600 last Ramadan.

## الجمهور · Audience
A person: name them, age range, city, household, what their day looks like, what they think about the category and the brand today, where and when they will meet the campaign. Secondary audience, if any, in one line.

## الإنسايت · Insight
One true, specific observation about the audience that creates tension or opportunity. Not a product fact.

## الرسالة الواحدة · Single-minded proposition
One sentence. The thing the audience must take away. No "and."

## النبرة · Tone
Three words. What it is not. Arabic register (فصحى / عربي أبيض / لهجة محلية) and Arabic-English mix. Reference to brand voice in the client file.

## الإلزاميات والقيود · Mandatories and constraints
- Brand: logo lockup, colors, fonts, templates
- Legal and sector: claims allowed and forbidden, disclaimers, licenses to show
- Cultural and religious: music, imagery, humor limits, gender representation rules agreed with the client
- Platform: format specs, text limits, prohibited content categories
- Partner or government identity guidelines, if any
