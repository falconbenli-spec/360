# Channel and Format Matrix

Assign each channel one role. A channel without a role is budget without a reason. Verify current platform specs before production; they change.

| Channel | Typical role | Strong formats | Notes for Saudi audiences |
|---|---|---|---|
| Snapchat | Awareness and reach among younger and family audiences; conversion with Snap Pixel | Vertical video 6–15s, Story ads, AR lenses, Commercials, Spotlight | Very high penetration in KSA; lenses and filters perform around occasions; sound-on common |
| TikTok | Awareness, creator-led consideration, discovery | Vertical video 9–21s, Spark Ads, TopView, creator content | Fast trend cycles; native-feeling content beats polished; Arabic captions |
| Instagram | Consideration, brand aesthetic, retail and lifestyle conversion | Reels, Stories, carousel, collection ads | Visual categories (fashion, beauty, F&B, real estate); shopping tags |
| X (Twitter) | Conversation, news, launches, customer service, reach for national moments | Promoted posts, video, trend takeover, Amplify | Saudi is one of X's most active markets; public sentiment forms here; crisis risk also here |
| YouTube | Long-form proof, how-to, storytelling, reach via Shorts | Bumper 6s, skippable 15–30s, Shorts, in-feed | Strong for consideration and product education; Arabic voice-over quality matters |
| Google Search | Capture intent, conversion | RSAs, Performance Max, Shopping | Arabic and English keyword sets separately; see arabic-seo |
| Meta (Facebook) | Older demographics, expat communities, groups | Feed, video, lead ads | Lower among young Saudis; useful for specific segments |
| LinkedIn | B2B consideration, employer brand, thought leadership | Document ads, video, conversation ads, thought leader ads | Decision makers in enterprises and government |

## Format planning rules

- Produce vertical first (9:16), then adapt to square and horizontal.
- Every video needs a sound-off version with Arabic captions and a 6-second cut.
- Arabic and English are separate creative pieces, not translations; right-to-left layouts are designed, not mirrored.
- One hero asset, then a modular system: backgrounds, product shots, lines, CTAs that recombine for testing (see ad-creative).
- Landing pages per campaign, bilingual, with tracking confirmed before launch.

## Budget split guidance

Set the split from the objective and the audience's platform mix, not from habit. State the reasoning in the media brief. Reserve a portion for testing and for mid-campaign reallocation to what works.
