# Campaign Timeline Template

Work backward from the launch date. Insert the client's approval windows from the client file. Seasonal campaigns start at least eight weeks before launch; TV, OOH, and large productions need more.

## Standard integrated campaign (8 weeks to launch)

| Week | Milestone | Owner | Output | Approval |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| −8 | Kickoff, ask interrogated, inputs collected | Agency + client | Signed-off brief | Client (brief) |
| −7 | Insight and proposition workshop; concept generation | Agency | Fifteen to twenty raw ideas, three routes shaped | Internal critique |
| −6 | Routes presented | Agency | Deck, hero references, cost bands | Client picks route |
| −5 | Creative development: scripts, key visuals, Arabic and English lines; media plan drafted | Agency | Scripts, KV, media plan, tracking plan | Client (scripts, KV, media plan) |
| −4 | Pre-production: talent, locations, permits, music, production partner; landing page build starts | Agency + production | Shooting board, schedule | Client (talent, locations) |
| −3 | Shoot and design production; copy for all formats | Production + agency | Raw footage, design system | — |
| −2 | Post-production, adaptations to all formats, captions, versions; tracking implemented and tested | Agency | Final assets, QA'd pages, test conversions | Client (final creative) |
| −1 | Campaign setup on platforms, OOH and TV materials delivered to vendors, influencer content approved, PR materials ready | Agency | Live-ready campaigns | Client (final sign-off) |
| 0 | Launch; daily monitoring for the first week | Agency | Launch confirmation, day-1 and day-3 checks | — |
| +2 | Mid-campaign review; reallocation; creative refresh if fatigue | Agency | Review note | Client (budget shifts if material) |
| End | Campaign closes; retargeting continues where planned | Agency | — | — |
| +2 weeks after end | Wrap-up report; learnings to client file | Agency | Report | — |

## Ramadan campaign (plan from 12 weeks before Ramadan)

- −12 weeks: brief and routes (dates verified for the current year)
- −9 weeks: route chosen; production planned around the client's own Ramadan operations
- −6 weeks: production
- −3 weeks: all creative approved; media booked (inventory tightens); influencer briefs out
- −1 week (late Sha'ban): everything live-ready; pre-Ramadan teaser if planned
- Ramadan week 1: launch; monitor after-iftar peaks
- Last ten nights: higher competition and CPMs; planned flighting
- Eid: separate creative switches on; Eid greeting content ready weeks earlier
- Post-Eid: wrap-up and retention follow-up

## Launch campaign phases

| Phase | Duration | Purpose | Main formats |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tease | 1–2 weeks | Curiosity, waitlist, press embargo | Short video, OOH teaser, influencer hints |
| Launch | 1–2 weeks | Reveal, offer, concentrated media | Hero film, PR, search, social, event |
| Sustain | 4–8 weeks | Proof, reviews, retargeting, always-on | Testimonials, UGC, retargeting, email |

## Approval buffer rule

Add the client's approval window to every milestone marked "Client." If the client's window is five business days and there are five approvals, the timeline carries five weeks of client time. Show this to the client at briefing; late approvals shift the launch day for day (see account-management).
