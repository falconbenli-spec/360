# Concept Generation Methods

Use several methods to produce fifteen to twenty raw ideas before judging. Write each idea as: name, one sentence, hero execution, the line (draft).

## 1. Dramatize the insight
Take the audience tension from the brief and make it the story. Show the moment the tension is sharpest, then the brand's role in resolving it.
Prompt: "Show the second before the problem, then the second after."

## 2. Flip the proposition
State the proposition, then state its opposite, then find the idea in the contrast. "Fast delivery" → "What would you do with the hour you got back?"

## 3. Ritual hijack
Find a ritual the audience already performs around the occasion or the category (the pre-iftar rush, the National Day drive, the Thursday night family dinner) and give the brand a role inside it, without changing it.

## 4. Enemy framing
Name what the brand is against: a habit, a hassle, a category convention, a feeling. The campaign fights the enemy, not the competitor. Keep it legal and respectful; no naming competitors.

## 5. Extreme demonstration
Push the product's main benefit to an absurd or theatrical degree to make it unforgettable. Works for functional benefits.

## 6. Cultural moment participation
Join a moment people already care about (season, event, trend) with a point of view only this brand could have. Pass the logo-cover test or drop it.

## 7. Format subversion
Use a familiar format in an unexpected way: a weather forecast, a receipt, a job ad, a wedding invitation, a government notice (careful), a sports commentary.

## 8. User as hero
The audience's own stories, content, or achievements become the campaign. Needs a mechanic for participation and moderation (see ugc-strategy if installed, and community-marketing).

## 9. Proof made visible
Turn a reason to believe into the whole idea: a live counter, a public test, a guarantee dramatized, a behind-the-scenes shown fully.

## 10. Local specificity
Make it unmistakably about this city or region: places, phrases, habits. Riyadh is not Jeddah is not Dammam. Dialect and reference choices come from arabic-copywriting and saudi-market.

## 11. Long-idea platform
Instead of a campaign, propose a platform the brand can run for years (a recurring event, a yearly ranking, a signature format). Present it as the bold route when the client has the appetite.

## 12. Constraint as idea
Take the biggest constraint (tiny budget, no shoot, one product, strict regulation) and make it the creative premise.

## Judging table

| Idea | On brief | One sentence | Logo-cover test | Extends to 10 | Doable | Safe (culture, religion, crisis) | Drives KPI | Keep? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Kill anything failing "On brief," "Logo-cover," or "Safe." Rank the rest.

## Shaping a route

For each kept idea write:
- **Name** (two or three words, memorable internally)
- **The idea** in one sentence
- **From which insight**
- **Hero execution**: the one asset that carries it (a 30-second film, an OOH takeover, a stunt, a product drop, an app feature)
- **Extensions**: three more executions across different channels
- **The line**: Arabic (written as Arabic, not translated) and English
- **Needs**: shoot, talent, locations, partners, tech
- **Cost band**: low / medium / high relative to the budget
- **Main risk** and mitigation
