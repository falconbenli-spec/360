## Landing Page Structure (mobile-first)

1. Hero: project name, one-line promise, location, starting instalment or price (verified), WhatsApp and call buttons.
2. Location: map with anchors (roads, schools, mosques, malls, metro), drive-times.
3. Unit mix: types, areas in m², starting prices, instalment examples with assumptions stated.
4. Payment plan table.
5. Amenities and lifestyle (renderings, video).
6. Delivery date and construction progress (update monthly).
7. Developer credentials and delivered projects.
8. Financing partners and Sakani eligibility (if confirmed).
9. FAQ (ownership, fees, handover, resale, maintenance).
10. Compliance footer: REGA licence number, Wafi permit, commercial registration.
11. Sticky bottom bar: WhatsApp, call, book a visit.

## WhatsApp Flow

Pre-filled message from ads and the page:
```
السلام عليكم، مهتم بمشروع [الاسم] - [كود المصدر]
```

Auto-acknowledgement (within a minute):
```
أهلاً بك في [المشروع]. سعيدين باهتمامك.
هذا ملف المشروع: [رابط]
بيتواصل معك أحد مستشارينا خلال ساعة. إذا تحب تحجز زيارة للمركز مباشرة: [رابط الحجز]
عشان نخدمك أسرع: وش نوع الوحدة اللي تهمك، وميزانيتك التقريبية؟
```

Agent first message (human, within the hour):
```
أهلاً [الاسم]، معك [اسم المستشار] من [المشروع]. شفت اهتمامك بـ[نوع الوحدة].
أقدر أتصل عليك الحين، أو تفضّل وقت معين؟
```

## Creative Angles

| Angle | Who | First-second message | Proof element |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instalment | First-home families | "من [X] ر.س شهرياً" (verified) | Financing partner logo, payment table |
| Location | All | Drive-times to anchors | Map, drone |
| Lifestyle | Upgraders | Amenities, privacy, design | Renderings, walkthrough |
| Certainty | Families, investors | Developer track record, delivery date, Wafi permit | Delivered projects, licence numbers |
| Investor | Investors | Location fundamentals, payment plan, exit options; no promised yields | Area data, developer history |
| Scarcity | Late phase only, verified weekly | "[N] وحدة متبقية" | Live availability |

## Launch Sequence

| Week | Activity |
|---|---|
| T-8 to T-5 | Assets in production; tracking and CRM design; broker outreach; media list |
| T-4 to T-2 | Teaser campaign: register your interest; database building; sales training; portal listings prepared |
| T-1 | Tracking verified end to end; dry run of WhatsApp and call routing; event logistics confirmed; press embargo set |
| Launch week | Announcement on all channels; launch event; priority access for registered leads; PR; peak paid budget |
| T+1 to T+4 | Always-on lead gen; retargeting; weekly creative refresh; broker activations; first funnel review at two weeks |
| T+5 to T+12 | Open days; price-list updates; nurture and reactivation; monthly report; model updated with actuals |
| Close-out | Verified last-units messaging; handover comms; owner referral program |

## Launch Event Checklist

- Venue: sales center or a hotel ballroom; prayer-time scheduling; separate family seating if appropriate for the audience.
- Registration linked to CRM; QR check-in; tagged source.
- Scale model or interactive screens; price list and reservation desk; financing partner desk.
- Agents briefed with scripts and the objection doc; a senior closer for hot leads.
- Content capture for social and PR (photo, video, short interviews).
- Follow-up within 24 hours to every attendee with the brochure and a visit booking link.
- Event KPIs: attendees, qualified conversations, reservations, cost per reservation.
