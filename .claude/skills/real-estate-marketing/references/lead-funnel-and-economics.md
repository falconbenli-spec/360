## Qualified Lead Definition

Agree in writing with the sales team before launch. Typical criteria (choose and set thresholds with the client):

| Criterion | Example threshold | Captured where |
|---|---|---|
| Budget band | At or above the lowest unit price with financing | Form field or first WhatsApp question |
| Intent | Buying for own use or investment within the timeline | First call |
| Timeline | Decision within 3 to 6 months | First call |
| Location fit | Wants this city and this area type | Form or first call |
| Financing | Eligible or has cash; Sakani status if relevant | First call |
| Reachable | Answered or replied within two attempts | CRM |

A lead that fails any hard criterion is "unqualified," with the reason recorded. Leads that cannot be reached after a defined number of attempts are "unreachable," reported separately because they are usually a tracking or speed issue, not an audience issue.

## Backward Budget Calculation

```
Units to sell in window                        U
Contracts needed                                = U
Reservations needed                             = U ÷ (reservation→contract rate)
Site visits needed                              = reservations ÷ (visit→reservation rate)
Qualified leads needed                          = visits ÷ (qualified→visit rate)
Leads needed                                    = qualified ÷ (lead→qualified rate)

Marketing budget (client sets as share of sales value or a fixed sum)   B
Allowable cost per lead                         = B ÷ leads needed
Allowable cost per qualified lead               = B ÷ qualified leads needed
Allowable cost per site visit                   = B ÷ site visits needed
```

Present the allowable costs to the client as the targets the campaign will be judged on. If early actual costs exceed them, the options are: improve capture and qualification, improve sales follow-up, shift channels, or revisit the budget. Do not simply keep spending.

## Lead Routing Rules

- Hot (budget confirmed, timeline under 3 months): senior agent, immediate call.
- Warm (budget fits, timeline 3 to 6 months): assigned agent, call within the hour, nurture sequence.
- Cool (timeline over 6 months or budget unclear): nurture sequence on WhatsApp, monthly check-in.
- Investor: dedicated investor contact with the investor deck; no instalment messaging.
- Broker-referred: tagged to the broker in the CRM to avoid commission disputes; the first-registration rule is written down.

Hand the CRM configuration to revops.
