# Audit Scorecard Rubric

Score each area 1 to 5. The score describes the current state against the client's objective, not against a perfect textbook setup. Weight analytics and tracking double when computing the overall score, since nothing else can be measured without it.

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1 | Absent or broken; actively costing money or leads |
| 2 | Exists but poorly set up; major gaps; results unreliable |
| 3 | Functional; obvious improvements available; no critical failures |
| 4 | Good; consistent; minor optimizations remain |
| 5 | Strong; a competitive advantage; maintain and extend |

## Area criteria

### Website and conversion
- 1: Site down, broken forms, no mobile usability, no clear action
- 2: Slow, confusing, weak value proposition, forms work but no follow-up
- 3: Clear enough, converts, some friction, trust elements thin
- 4: Fast, clear, strong paths, bilingual parity, trust visible
- 5: Tested and optimized continuously, measured conversion paths, excellent mobile and Arabic

### SEO and content
- 1: Not indexed or heavily penalized; no content
- 2: Indexed with technical problems; thin content; no rankings beyond brand
- 3: Technically sound; some non-brand rankings; content irregular
- 4: Clear strategy; growing rankings; local SEO maintained; hreflang right
- 5: Category authority; content engine; AI search presence

### Paid media
- 1: No tracking, broad waste, no structure, ads months old
- 2: Running but structure and conversion actions wrong; wasted spend visible
- 3: Reasonable structure; tracking mostly right; limited testing
- 4: Clean structure; verified conversions; regular creative testing; budget by performance
- 5: Full-funnel, incrementality-aware, creative pipeline, cross-platform budget decisions

### Social (organic)
- 1: Dormant or inconsistent brand presentation
- 2: Posting irregularly; promotional only; low engagement; no replies
- 3: Consistent posting; some value content; replies within a day
- 4: Clear content pillars; video-led; community engaged; UGC appears
- 5: Social drives measurable demand; creator ecosystem; distinctive voice

### Email, messaging, CRM
- 1: No CRM; leads in inboxes; no consent process
- 2: CRM exists but marketing data does not reach it; no automated flows
- 3: Basic flows; list growing; routing works; authentication set
- 4: Lifecycle flows; segmentation; speed to lead measured; WhatsApp integrated
- 5: Revenue attributed to lifecycle; experimentation on flows; clean data

### Brand and messaging
- 1: No consistent identity; contradictory messages
- 2: Logo consistent but messaging generic and interchangeable with competitors
- 3: Clear positioning on the site; inconsistent elsewhere
- 4: Consistent positioning, voice, and visuals across touchpoints; proof used
- 5: Distinctive; recognized; defended by evidence; guides every channel

### Analytics and tracking (double weight)
- 1: No analytics or pixels; conversions not tracked
- 2: Installed but misconfigured; conversions wrong or duplicated; no UTMs
- 3: Core conversions tracked; some gaps (calls, WhatsApp); reports exist
- 4: Full funnel tracked; CAPI; UTM discipline; reconciled monthly
- 5: Attribution model agreed; dashboards used in decisions; data quality monitored

### Competitor position
- 1: Clearly behind on every visible dimension
- 2: Behind on most; no awareness of competitor activity
- 3: At par on most dimensions
- 4: Ahead on key dimensions; monitoring competitors
- 5: Category leader; competitors follow

## Overall score

Overall = (sum of area scores + analytics score) ÷ (number of areas scored + 1). State which areas were not scored for lack of access and exclude them from the denominator.
