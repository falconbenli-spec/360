## Review Response Templates (adapt to the brand's voice)

Positive review:
```
شكراً [الاسم]، سعدنا بزيارتك. ننتظرك قريباً في فرع [الفرع].
```

Complaint about food quality:
```
نعتذر لك [الاسم]، هذا مو المستوى اللي نشتغل عليه. أرسلنا لك رسالة خاصة عشان نعرف التفاصيل ونعوضك.
تم تنبيه فريق فرع [الفرع] اليوم.
```

Complaint about service or wait time:
```
نعتذر عن التأخير [الاسم]. راجعنا الموضوع مع فريق الفرع وعدّلنا [الإجراء]. نتمنى تعطينا فرصة ثانية.
```

Complaint about delivery (order via app):
```
نعتذر [الاسم]، وصلنا ملاحظتك. تواصلنا مع [التطبيق] بخصوص طلبك، وراسلناك على الخاص عشان نحل الموضوع.
```

Rules: never argue, never blame the guest, never mention a competitor, never offer rewards publicly for changing a rating.

## Local Paid Setup

| Channel | Objective | Targeting | Creative | Measure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Google Local campaigns / Performance Max for store goals | Directions, calls | Radius per branch | Branch photos, offer | Direction requests, calls, store visits where available |
| Snapchat | Awareness and visits | Radius or district; age bands per concept | Vertical video of the hero product and space; map pin | Swipe-ups to directions or delivery link; code redemptions |
| TikTok | Discovery | City, interest in food and cafés | Creator or in-house vertical video | Views to profile, link clicks, code redemptions |
| Instagram | Brand layer | City | Reels, stories | Profile visits, saves, DMs |

Set radius by branch type: roughly the delivery radius for delivery-led branches, and the realistic drive-time catchment for dine-in. Verify with order data rather than assuming.

## Opening Checklist

| When | Item | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| T-6w | Google Business Profile created as "opening soon" with date; delivery-app onboarding started | Agency / client ops |
| T-6w | Site hoarding with brand, date, QR to WhatsApp list or loyalty signup | Agency (offline-media) |
| T-5w | Creator long-list for the city; briefs drafted | Agency (influencer-marketing) |
| T-4w | Social teasers start; local community pages and nearby offices or compounds contacted | Agency (social) |
| T-3w | Menu, photos, and prices final across Google, apps, in-store | Client ops / agency |
| T-2w | Opening offer designed with contribution check; codes created; POS ready | Client finance / agency |
| T-2w | Staff training complete; operations readiness sign-off | Client ops |
| T-1w | Soft-opening guest list invited (database, creators, neighbours) | Agency |
| Soft opening (3–7 days) | Feedback collected; fixes applied; honest review requests | Client ops / agency |
| Opening day | Creators posting; geo-targeted paid live; Google profile switched to open; app listings live | Agency |
| Week 1–2 | Reason-to-return offer; daily review monitoring; reply within 24 hours | Agency |
| Week 4 | Second creator wave; loyalty push; first performance review | Agency |
| Week 8 | Limited-time item launch; review-theme fixes confirmed | Agency / client ops |
| Day 90 | Full review: transactions by channel, Google actions, app orders, repeat rate, reviews; plan for the next quarter | Agency |

## Footfall Measurement Options

| Method | What it measures | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| POS transactions by hour | Paying guests | Best primary measure; split by channel |
| Door counters or Wi-Fi analytics | Entries | Counts staff and non-buyers; use for trend |
| Google Business Profile direction requests and calls | Intent | Leading indicator |
| Delivery-app orders | Delivery demand | Per app, per branch |
| Redemption codes per campaign | Campaign-driven visits | Requires POS discipline |
| Reservation or waitlist tools | Dine-in demand | Where used |
| Loyalty check-ins | Identified repeat visits | Depends on adoption |

Agree the primary measure per branch before launching anything that promises footfall.
