# Delivery Apps Playbook

Apps change their rules, fees, and ranking factors often. Everything here is a working model to confirm with each app's merchant account manager and the client's contracts.

## App Landscape in Saudi

| App | Notes to verify with the client |
|---|---|
| HungerStation | Long-established; broad coverage; merchant promotions and sponsored placements available |
| Jahez | Strong in several cities; merchant campaigns; own logistics |
| Keeta | Newer entrant; aggressive promotions during expansion; terms may differ |
| ToYou | Multi-category delivery; F&B and retail |
| Mrsool | Courier and marketplace model; useful for items outside standard menus |
| The Chefz, Marsool-type niche apps, mall and grocery apps | Concept-dependent |

Pick apps by the branch's catchment demand, the commission, and the operational load. More apps is not always better; each adds tablets, prep-time pressure, and promotion asks.

## Listing Standards

- Full menu synced with in-store; Arabic names first, short descriptions that say what is in it and the portion.
- Photo for every item, consistent style, hero items first.
- Categories and tags correct (cuisine, dietary, family meals, breakfast).
- Prep times set honestly per item type; update during peaks (iftar).
- Hours correct including Ramadan and holidays; pause when the kitchen cannot deliver rather than accepting and cancelling.
- Bundles and family boxes built for delivery margin and travel quality (packaging tested).

## Ranking Inputs to Manage

Confirm with each app which of these matter and how much:

| Input | Owner | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Rating | Operations | Fix the top complaint themes; packaging and accuracy first |
| Acceptance rate | Operations | Pause the store rather than reject |
| Prep-time accuracy | Operations | Realistic times; peak adjustments |
| Cancellation rate | Operations | Stock sync; accurate menu availability |
| Order volume | Marketing and operations | Promotions, hero bundles, own-channel cross-promotion |
| Promotion participation | Marketing | Only with a contribution check |
| Sponsored placement | Marketing | Test by branch and daypart with a cost per incremental order target |

Share this table with the client: most ranking is earned in the kitchen.

## Promotion Economics Model

Fill per promotion and per app:

```
Menu price                                   P
less app commission (rate from contract)     P × c
less merchant-funded discount                D
less packaging and delivery-related costs    K
less food cost                               F
= contribution per promoted order            CP

Baseline contribution per order (no promo)   CB
Baseline orders per week                     OB
Expected orders per week during promo        OP (from past promos; label approximate)

Net weekly change = (OP × CP) − (OB × CB)
```

Run a promotion that loses contribution only with a stated purpose and an end date: a new branch, a new app launch, a slow daypart test, or a ranking push. Report the net change afterwards and log it in Learnings.

## Own-Channel Ordering Strategy

- Build or adopt an own ordering channel (website, app, WhatsApp ordering) where the volume justifies it; commission saved funds a guest incentive (free item, points) and the guest data is captured.
- Promote it on packaging inserts, receipts, Google Business Profile, social bios, and in app packaging ("اطلب من موقعنا المرة الجاية واحصل على [الميزة]").
- Keep app listings active for discovery; move repeat guests to own channels.
- Track the share of orders by channel monthly; it is the strategic metric.
