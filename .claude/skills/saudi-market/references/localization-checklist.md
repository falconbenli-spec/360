# Saudi Localization Checklist

Run this before launching anything aimed at a Saudi or GCC audience. Each item is a yes, no, or not applicable, with an owner. "No" on a compliance item blocks launch.

## 1. Language and Copy

- [ ] Arabic is the default language on the page, app, ad, or email; English is a switch, not a fallback.
- [ ] Register chosen deliberately (MSA, white Arabic, or Saudi dialect) and consistent across the funnel. See arabic-copywriting.
- [ ] Gender address decided (masculine plural default, feminine for women-targeted, or neutral) and applied consistently.
- [ ] Copy was written or transcreated in Arabic, not translated word for word. No machine-translation tells.
- [ ] Numerals policy set (Western digits 0-9 are standard in Saudi marketing; Eastern Arabic digits only if brand style requires) and applied.
- [ ] Prices in SAR with the currency symbol or "ر.س" or "ريال" consistently; VAT-inclusive for consumers.
- [ ] Dates shown in Gregorian with Hijri where relevant (government, religious occasions).
- [ ] Phone numbers in +966 format; addresses follow the Saudi National Address format (building number, street, district, city, postal code, additional number).
- [ ] Legal, privacy, and terms pages available in Arabic.

## 2. Design and RTL

- [ ] Full right-to-left layout, not just right-aligned text: navigation, icons with direction (arrows, chevrons), progress bars, carousels, and form labels all mirrored.
- [ ] Arabic font chosen for legibility at small sizes (e.g. IBM Plex Arabic, Noto Naskh/Kufi Arabic, Tajawal, Cairo, Almarai, or a licensed brand font); line height increased for Arabic.
- [ ] Mixed Arabic and English strings render correctly (bidi issues, brand names, numbers inside Arabic sentences).
- [ ] Imagery shows Saudi people, places, and context authentically; modest dress norms respected; regional variety considered.
- [ ] No imagery that conflicts with religious or cultural norms (alcohol, pork, gambling, inappropriate dress, daytime eating during Ramadan).
- [ ] Payment logos: Mada, Apple Pay, Visa/Mastercard, STC Pay, Tamara and Tabby where offered.

## 3. Trust Signals

- [ ] Commercial registration number visible (footer, checkout).
- [ ] Maroof listing or equivalent trust badge for e-commerce.
- [ ] Saudi phone and WhatsApp support with hours and Arabic-speaking agents.
- [ ] Delivery times per city stated honestly; returns policy in Arabic.
- [ ] Saudi or GCC testimonials, reviews, logos, and case studies present.
- [ ] Physical address (if any) shown.

## 4. Compliance

- [ ] PDPL: consent captured for marketing messages; privacy notice in Arabic; data processing purposes stated; unsubscribe works; no purchased lists.
- [ ] SMS: registered sender ID through a licensed aggregator; opt-out honored.
- [ ] WhatsApp: opt-in recorded; approved templates used for business-initiated messages.
- [ ] Promotions: Ministry of Commerce discount permit obtained where required; original prices genuine; terms displayed.
- [ ] Influencers: Mawthooq license verified; paid partnership disclosed in the post.
- [ ] Sector approvals: health (MoH/SFDA), finance (SAMA/CMA), real estate (REGA/Fal), education, and others as applicable.
- [ ] No prohibited content: alcohol, gambling, dating, adult, mocking religion, political statements, unlicensed use of religious text.
- [ ] Advertising claims substantiated; comparative claims defensible; superlatives avoided unless proven.
- [ ] Trademark and brand name registered or cleared in Saudi (SAIP).

## 5. Channel Setup

- [ ] Ad accounts set to Saudi Arabia with SAR billing where possible; time zone set to Riyadh (UTC+3).
- [ ] Audiences built on Saudi geography and Arabic language, with city-level splits for Riyadh, Jeddah, and Eastern Province where budgets allow.
- [ ] Creative formats are platform-native: vertical video for Snapchat and TikTok, Arabic captions, sound-on and sound-off versions.
- [ ] Ad scheduling weighted to evening; Ramadan schedule adjusted separately.
- [ ] Google Business Profile in Arabic and English with Saudi address; see arabic-seo.
- [ ] hreflang ar-SA and en-SA (or ar and en) configured; see arabic-seo.
- [ ] Customer care covering X, Instagram DMs, Snapchat, and WhatsApp during campaign hours, with escalation for public complaints.

## 6. Calendar and Timing

- [ ] Campaign dates checked against Ramadan, Eid, Hajj, National Day, Founding Day, exam seasons, and Riyadh Season.
- [ ] Creative variants prepared for moon-sighting uncertainty around Eid.
- [ ] Pause plan in place for national mourning or crisis events.
- [ ] Customer service and fulfillment staffed for holiday peaks.

## 7. Measurement

- [ ] Analytics and conversion tracking consented and working in Arabic pages; see conversion-tracking.
- [ ] Baseline period defined before seasonal lift is claimed.
- [ ] Saudi-specific KPIs set (Arabic-language conversion rate, Mada share, WhatsApp lead response time, city-level CAC).
- [ ] Learnings logged to `.agents/product-marketing.md` after the campaign.
