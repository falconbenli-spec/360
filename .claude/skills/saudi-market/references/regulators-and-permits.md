# Saudi Regulators and Marketing Permits Quick Reference

This is an orientation map, not legal advice. Requirements change; confirm with the regulator's current guidance or legal counsel before launch.

| Area | Regulator | What marketers need | Typical trigger |
|---|---|---|---|
| Personal data, consent, cookies, data transfer | SDAIA (Saudi Data and AI Authority) under the Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) | Lawful basis and consent for marketing, Arabic privacy notice, data subject request handling, records of processing, cross-border transfer rules, possible registration | Any lead form, email/SMS/WhatsApp marketing, pixels, CRM |
| SMS and telecom marketing | CST (Communications, Space and Technology Commission) | Registered sender ID via licensed aggregator, opt-out compliance, content rules | Promotional SMS, OTP, WhatsApp via telecom partners |
| Advertising content and influencers | GCAM (General Commission for Audiovisual Media) | Advertising content standards; Mawthooq license for paid social media advertisers (individuals); disclosure of paid content | Influencer deals, video ads, audiovisual campaigns |
| Promotions, discounts, e-commerce, misleading ads | Ministry of Commerce | Discount and promotion permit; honest pricing; E-Commerce Law obligations (seller identity, Arabic terms, returns); commercial registration display; Maroof for online stores | Sales events, coupons, online stores, comparative claims |
| Consumer prices and tax | ZATCA (Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority) | VAT-inclusive consumer pricing; e-invoicing (Fatoora) for sales | Any price display, checkout, invoices |
| Health, medicines, supplements, cosmetics, food claims | Ministry of Health; SFDA (Saudi Food and Drug Authority) | Pre-approval of medical and health advertising; registered products only; restricted claims | Clinics, pharma, supplements, cosmetics, food health claims |
| Banking, lending, BNPL, insurance, investment | SAMA (Saudi Central Bank); CMA (Capital Market Authority) | Licensed entities only; no guaranteed returns; mandatory disclosures; approved wording | Fintech, BNPL, insurance, investment platforms |
| Real estate marketing | REGA (Real Estate General Authority) | Fal license for brokers and marketers; ad permit numbers displayed on listings | Property listings, developer campaigns |
| Education and training | Ministry of Education; TVTC | Licensed institutions; approved program claims | Courses, schools, training ads |
| Tourism and events | Ministry of Tourism; GEA (General Entertainment Authority) | Event permits; licensed tourism operators | Events, activations, travel packages |
| Trademarks | SAIP (Saudi Authority for Intellectual Property) | Trademark registration and clearance | Brand names, slogans, campaign names |
| Charity and fundraising | Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development; National Platform for Donations (Ehsan) | Only licensed charities may fundraise; cause marketing must partner with licensed entities | Ramadan charity campaigns, CSR |
| Public procurement | Etimad platform (Ministry of Finance) | Vendor registration; local content requirements | Government tenders, G2B |

## Permit Workflow Template

1. List every claim, offer, channel, and partner in the campaign.
2. Map each to the table above.
3. Assign an owner and a deadline for each permit or verification (promotion permits and sector approvals can take weeks).
4. Store permit numbers and license screenshots in the campaign folder; many must appear in the ad itself (e.g. real estate ad permit number, promotion permit reference).
5. Re-check when creative changes; a new claim can need a new approval.
