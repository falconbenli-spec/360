# Saudi and GCC Seasonal Marketing Calendar

Hijri dates move about 11 days earlier each Gregorian year. Confirm the exact dates for the campaign year from the Umm al-Qura calendar before booking anything. Planning lead times below are practical minimums for creative, approvals, and media booking; TV and OOH during Ramadan and National Day need longer.

## Annual Overview

| Moment | When | Themes that work | Categories that peak | Lead time | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Founding Day (يوم التأسيس) | 22 Feb | Heritage, Najdi history, traditional dress, authenticity, "roots" | Fashion (traditional), F&B, government, telecom, banks | 6 weeks | Fireworks-style National Day clichés; fabricated history |
| Pre-Ramadan | 3 to 4 weeks before Ramadan | Preparation, home, kitchen, grocery stock-up, dates and coffee, Ramadan décor | Grocery, home, appliances, kitchenware, charity | 10 to 12 weeks for creative, earlier for TV | Launching new unrelated products the week before |
| Ramadan (رمضان) | Hijri month 9 | Family, generosity, reflection, togetherness, iftar and suhoor, charity (zakat, sadaqah), Ramadan series | F&B, grocery, charity, TV, home, telecom data bundles, gold and jewelry (late), fashion (late) | 12 weeks | Daytime food imagery, loud or party creative, daytime-heavy ad scheduling |
| Last 10 nights of Ramadan | End of month | Charity, worship, Eid prep, gifting | Fashion, perfume, gifting, travel | Part of Ramadan plan | New launches, hard-sell |
| Eid al-Fitr (عيد الفطر) | 1 Shawwal, 3 to 4 days | Celebration, new clothes, Eidiyah (cash gifts), visiting family, travel, sweets | Fashion, perfume, gifting, restaurants, domestic travel, entertainment | Creative ready before the final week of Ramadan | Going dark; expecting B2B activity |
| Post-Eid lull | 2 weeks after Eid | Return to routine, fitness restart, finance reset | Fitness, finance, education | 4 weeks | Heavy spend; audiences are travelling |
| Hajj season and Eid al-Adha (عيد الأضحى) | Dhu al-Hijjah (months 12); Eid on the 10th | Sacrifice, family gatherings, travel, meat and grocery, pilgrimage hospitality | Grocery, travel, Makkah and Madinah hospitality, gifting | 8 weeks | Commercializing Hajj rituals; irreverent humor |
| Summer (June to August) | Gregorian | Travel abroad, indoor entertainment, AC and appliances, summer camps, long evenings | Travel, malls, entertainment, electronics, kids activities | 6 weeks | Outdoor events in daytime heat |
| Back to school (العودة للمدارس) | Late August | Supplies, uniforms, laptops, tutoring, routines | Retail, electronics, education, F&B lunch | 6 weeks | Starting after schools open |
| Saudi National Day (اليوم الوطني) | 23 Sep (holiday may extend around it) | National pride, green, Saudi achievements, year-number offers (e.g. 94 SAR for the 94th), employee celebrations | Every category; biggest retail and brand moment | 8 to 10 weeks; OOH and TV book much earlier | Generic flags with no product link; copying the same line as every brand; errors in the year number |

| 11.11 and Singles' Day sales | 11 Nov | Marketplace discounts | E-commerce (noon, Amazon.sa) | 6 weeks | Fake discounts (Ministry of Commerce enforces) |

| Sports moments | Year-round | Saudi Pro League, King's Cup, boxing and F1 events in Jeddah, national team matches | F&B, telecom, apparel, betting is prohibited | Reactive; prepare templates | Anything that implies gambling |

## Weekly and Daily Rhythm

- **Weekend** is Friday and Saturday. Thursday evening is the start of leisure time; Friday midday is prayer and family lunch; Saturday is shopping and errands.
- **Government and corporate hours** start early (around 7:30 to 8:00) and end mid-afternoon; Thursday is lighter. B2B outreach lands best Sunday to Wednesday mornings.
- **Consumer attention** peaks after Isha prayer through late night. Evening posting and evening ad weighting outperform morning in most B2C categories; verify with your own data.
- **Ramadan daily rhythm** flips: low engagement until mid-afternoon, a spike before iftar, and heavy late-night consumption through suhoor.
- **Payday**: many salaries land around the 25th to 27th of the month. Retail and BNPL conversion rises in the days after.

## Planning Template

For each chosen moment, fill in:

- Moment and confirmed dates
- Objective (awareness, launch, promotion, retention)
- Creative theme and the product link (why this brand belongs here)
- Channels and media booked, with booking deadlines
- Compliance: promotion permit, influencer licenses, sector approvals
- Offer mechanics and stock plan
- Customer service staffing (holidays reduce capacity; peaks increase tickets)
- Measurement: baseline period, lift metric, and the comparison window

## Notes on Religious Calendar Risk

- Eid dates are confirmed by moon sighting one or two days before. Build creative for both possible dates and hold the final switch until the announcement.
- Avoid scheduling product launches, webinars, or press releases on the first days of Eid, during Hajj, or on National Day itself unless they are part of the moment.
- Funeral or national mourning announcements can pause campaigns; keep a pause plan and a point of contact who can stop paid media within an hour.
