# Saudi Local SEO Checklist

## Google Business Profile (نشاطي التجاري)

- [ ] Profile claimed and verified for each physical location (and service-area settings for delivery or on-site services).
- [ ] Business name in Arabic as primary, matching signage and the commercial registration; English name added where the interface allows a second language or in the description. No keyword stuffing in the name.
- [ ] Primary category is the most specific available; secondary categories added.
- [ ] Address in the Saudi National Address format: building number, street, district (حي), city, postal code, additional number. Pin placed precisely on the entrance.
- [ ] Phone in +966 format; WhatsApp number listed where supported; website link to the Arabic page for Arabic-speaking locations.
- [ ] Opening hours accurate, including Friday hours and any prayer-time closures; special hours set for Ramadan, Eid, National Day.
- [ ] Arabic description (750 characters) with services, districts served, and a plain CTA; English description if the audience is mixed.
- [ ] Photos: exterior with Arabic signage, interior, team, products; refreshed monthly. Arabic file captions where possible.
- [ ] Products and services added with SAR prices (VAT-inclusive).
- [ ] Google Posts in Arabic for offers and seasonal moments.
- [ ] Q&A seeded with the real questions customers ask in Arabic; answered by the owner.
- [ ] Messaging enabled with Arabic-speaking responders during business hours.

## Reviews

- [ ] Review request sent by WhatsApp after each completed order or visit, with the direct review link and a one-line Arabic ask.
- [ ] Every review answered in Arabic within a few days; negative reviews answered calmly with a path to resolve offline.
- [ ] No incentives for reviews and no purchased reviews.
- [ ] Review keywords (services, districts) occur naturally because customers mention them, not because they are prompted to.

## Citations and NAP

- [ ] Name, address, and phone identical (character for character, including Arabic spelling) on the website footer, GBP, Maroof listing, Apple Maps, Snapchat and Instagram profiles, and any directory used.
- [ ] Apple Maps listing claimed via Apple Business Connect.
- [ ] Snap Map business location set; relevant for cafes, restaurants, retail with young audiences.
- [ ] Directory submissions limited to ones with real traffic or authority; verify current relevance before spending time (see directory-submissions).

## Website Local Signals

- [ ] LocalBusiness or the specific subtype schema on the location page, Arabic name, Saudi address, "addressCountry": "SA", phone, hours, geo coordinates, "sameAs" to GBP and social profiles.
- [ ] Embedded map on the contact page; address in Arabic and English text (not only an image).
- [ ] City and district pages with unique content: local team, delivery times, local proof, nearby landmarks, hours. No swapped-city duplicates.
- [ ] Arabic FAQ answering "هل توصلون إلى [district]؟" style questions.

## Tracking

- [ ] GBP performance (calls, direction requests, website clicks, messages) exported monthly.
- [ ] UTM on the GBP website link to separate GBP traffic in analytics.
- [ ] Rank tracking for "near me" and city-modified Arabic terms at the city level (Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam/Khobar), mobile.
