## Titles and Naming

- Use the entity's official Arabic and English names in full on first mention; approved short forms after.
- Officials: title, then name, exactly as the entity's protocol office provides (معالي، سعادة، صاحب السمو، etc.). Confirm each time; titles change.
- Programs and services: official names only, no invented abbreviations.
- Vision 2030 and its programs: official names in both languages.

## Turnaround Planning

Work back from the required publish date:

```
Publish date
 − layer 6 turnaround (if applicable)
 − layer 5 turnaround
 − parallel window for layers 2–4
 − consolidation by account contact
 − agency revision round (plan for two)
 − agency production
 = latest start date
```

If the latest start date is in the past, say so immediately and propose what can be cut or fast-tracked, in writing.

## Change Control

- Any addition beyond the contract's deliverables: write a short change note (what, why, effort, cost or offset, timeline impact), send to the account contact, and proceed only on written approval.
- Log verbal requests in the same day's email summary: "As discussed, you asked for X; we will proceed once confirmed."
- At month end, reconcile delivered items against the SLA and the change log in the report.
