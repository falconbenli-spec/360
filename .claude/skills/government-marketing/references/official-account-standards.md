# Official Account Standards

Apply when the agency manages or supports an entity's official social media accounts.

## Contents
- Governance
- Content calendar and approval
- Register and style
- Occasions and templates
- Reply and engagement policy
- Security
- Incident escalation
- Monthly standards audit

## Governance

- Written scope: which accounts, which activities (drafting, publishing, replying, reporting), hours of coverage, and who at the entity owns the accounts.
- Named agency staff with access; access removed the day a person leaves the account team.
- The entity retains ownership of accounts, handles, and credentials.

## Content Calendar and Approval

- Calendar submitted on the cadence in the SLA (weekly or monthly) with, for each post: date and time, channel, Arabic text, English text, visual, link, compliance sheet, and the source for any figure.
- Approved calendar is locked; changes go through the account contact with a version note.
- Reactive posts (announcements, occasions, responses) use pre-approved templates where possible and go through an expedited approval path defined in the protocol sheet.
- Two-person rule: drafter and checker are different people; the checker signs the compliance sheet.

## Register and Style

- Formal, clear Modern Standard Arabic. No dialect unless the entity's guidelines allow it for specific public campaigns.
- English rendering of each post; identical meaning.
- No emojis, no exclamation marks, no humour at anyone's expense, no engagement bait ("علّق بـ...").
- Hashtags: official campaign hashtags and national occasion hashtags only; follow the entity's list.
- Titles and names exact; organisations' official names; programs' official names.
- Numbers sourced and cited; percentages with the base stated.
- Imagery per the identity guideline; no third-party images without rights.
- Accessibility: alt text on every image; captions on every video; readable type on visuals.

## Occasions and Templates

Maintain approved templates (Arabic and English, visual) for:

- National Day (23 September), Founding Day (22 February), Flag Day (11 March).
- Ramadan start, Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha, Hajj season, Islamic New Year (dates confirmed annually against the official calendar).
- Entity-specific days (its establishment, its sector's international days).
- Condolence and mourning templates, used only on the entity's instruction.

Rules: confirm the exact date each year; post at the entity's approved time; no commercial tone; no mixing an occasion with a promotion.

## Reply and Engagement Policy

| Inbound type | Action | Window (per SLA) |
|---|---|---|
| Service question | Answer with approved information or route to the official service channel | Within SLA hours |
| Complaint about a service | Acknowledge, request details privately, route to the responsible department; track to closure where the scope includes it | Within SLA hours |
| Complaint about the entity's policy | Acknowledge receipt; no debate; route per the standard line | Within SLA hours |
| Praise | Thank briefly, or no reply, per the entity's practice | |
| Misinformation about the entity | Escalate to the entity; publish a correction only on approval | Immediate escalation |
| Abuse, hate, spam | Do not engage; hide or report per the entity's policy; log | |
| Political, sectarian, foreign-relations content | Never engage; log; escalate if it targets the entity | |
| Media inquiry via DM | Route to the entity's media office; do not answer | Same day |

Reply texts use approved templates; new wording goes through approval.

## Security

- Two-factor authentication on every account; recovery contacts owned by the entity.
- Access through a management platform with named users; no shared passwords in chat or email.
- Quarterly access review; immediate revocation on staff change.
- Device and session hygiene for anyone with access; no public Wi-Fi publishing.
- A written procedure for suspected compromise: who to call at the entity and the platform, how to lock the account.

## Incident Escalation

Any of the following goes to the entity's account owner immediately and to the crisis-communications process, with nothing posted in the meantime:

- A post with an error after publishing (factual, identity, language).
- A trending negative hashtag involving the entity.
- A complaint gaining reposts from large accounts.
- A suspected account compromise.
- A national incident or mourning period beginning (pause scheduled content pending instruction).

The agency never deletes an official post without instruction; a correction is posted the way the entity decides.
