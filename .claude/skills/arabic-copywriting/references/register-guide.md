# Arabic Register Decision Guide

Choosing the register is the first decision in any Arabic copy task. This guide gives the decision procedure, examples of the same message in each register, and the regional dialect notes for Saudi Arabia.

## Decision Procedure

1. **Who reads it?** Saudi nationals only: dialect or white Arabic are open. GCC-wide: white Arabic, with Gulf dialect touches. Pan-Arab: white Arabic or MSA; no Saudi dialect in the body.
2. **Where does it appear?** Snapchat, TikTok, X, WhatsApp, radio, creator scripts: dialect or white Arabic with dialect. Website, app, email, product pages: white Arabic. Legal, finance, government, press: MSA.
3. **What is the category's formality?** Banking, insurance, healthcare, government services, and B2B enterprise lean formal even on social. F&B, fashion, entertainment, telecom promotions, and consumer apps lean casual.
4. **What does the brand already sound like?** Match existing approved Arabic. Do not change a brand's register inside one campaign without a decision from the client.
5. **Is there humor?** Humor needs dialect. MSA jokes do not land.

Write the decision as one line: "Register: white Arabic, Najdi hook in hero, MSA legal line. Address: masculine singular."

## The Same Message in Four Registers

Brief: a grocery delivery app delivers in under an hour in Riyadh, first order free delivery.

**MSA (الفصحى)**
> توصيل طلبات البقالة في الرياض خلال أقل من ساعة. التوصيل مجاني على طلبك الأول.
> اطلب الآن

Use for: press release, government partnership page, app store description if the brand is formal.

**White Arabic (العربية البيضاء)**
> بقالتك توصلك في أقل من ساعة في الرياض، والتوصيل مجاني على أول طلب.
> اطلب الآن

Use for: landing page hero, email, in-app banner, Instagram feed caption.

**Saudi dialect (اللهجة السعودية، نجدي)**
> بقالتك عند بابك بأقل من ساعة، وأول طلب التوصيل علينا.
> اطلب الحين

Use for: Snapchat and TikTok ad, X post, WhatsApp broadcast, radio spot.

**Hejazi touch (جدة)**
> بقالتك توصلك لحد البيت في أقل من ساعة، وأول طلب التوصيل علينا.
> اطلب دحين

Use for: Jeddah-specific campaign. "دحين" (now) and "لحد" are Hejazi markers; "الحين" is Najdi and general Gulf.

## Vocabulary Markers by Register

| Meaning | MSA | White Arabic | Saudi dialect (Najdi) | Hejazi |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| now | الآن | الآن / حالًا | الحين | دحين |
| very | جدًا | جدًا / كثير | مرّة / وايد (Gulf) | مرّة |
| what | ماذا | ماذا / ما | وش / إيش | إيش |
| how | كيف | كيف | كيف / شلون (Gulf) | كيف |
| why | لماذا | لماذا / ليش | ليش | ليش |
| good | جيد | جيد / حلو | زين | حلو / كويس |
| want | أريد | أريد / أبغى | أبغى / أبي | أبغى |
| go | اذهب | روح | روح | روح |
| here | هنا | هنا | هنا / هني | هنا |
| okay | حسنًا | تمام | تمام / أبشر | تمام |
| free (no cost) | مجاني | مجاني / ببلاش | ببلاش / مجاني | ببلاش |
| this | هذا | هذا | ذا / هذا | هذا / ده (Egyptian influence, avoid) |

Use markers sparingly. One or two dialect words carry the voice; a line stuffed with them reads as a caricature.

## Common Register Failures

- **Ministry voice on Snapchat**: "نقدم لكم خدماتنا المتميزة" in a 6-second vertical ad. Fix: one dialect line and a verb.
- **Slang on a bank page**: "أبشر، فلوسك عندنا" on a savings product. Fix: white Arabic, calm, specific.
- **Egyptian in Saudi**: "عايز توصيل بسرعة؟" to a Riyadh audience. Fix: "تبغى توصيل سريع؟"
- **Register drift within one page**: dialect hero, MSA body, dialect CTA, MSA error messages. Fix: plan the register per section and keep UI strings in the body register.
- **Dialect in a pan-Arab campaign**: Saudi dialect on a GCC-plus-Egypt campaign. Fix: white Arabic body, optional per-country hooks.

## Gender Address Quick Table

| Audience | Imperative example | Possessive example |
|---|---|---|
| Mixed, formal | سجّلوا الآن | حسابكم |
| Mixed, direct (common default) | سجّل الآن | حسابك |
| Women | سجّلي الآن | حسابكِ |
| Women, plural | سجّلن الآن (MSA) / سجّلوا (common usage) | حسابكن (MSA) |
| Neutral | التسجيل مجاني | الحساب |

Note: in casual Saudi usage, women-targeted plural copy often uses the masculine plural form ("سجّلوا") because the MSA feminine plural ("سجّلن") sounds formal. Feminine singular ("سجّلي") is the clearest women-targeted choice.
